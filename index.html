About

I work at the intersection of AI, healthcare, education, scientific research, sustainability, and systems design.

My current focus includes CANary, AiR, DRISE Foundation, Dhara Tattva, and long-term research exploring accessible technology and intelligent systems designed for large-scale human impact.

Selected Work

CANary

AI-driven healthcare research focused on accessible early-stage cancer detection systems and long-term medical accessibility. The project explores how intelligent systems can make preventive healthcare more scalable, affordable, and accessible beyond traditional infrastructure.

AiR

A futuristic wearable AI ecosystem exploring real-time translation, intelligent assistance, gesture controls, health monitoring, and seamless human-AI interaction through accessible wearable computing systems.

DRISE Foundation

An education-focused initiative working toward literacy, accessible learning, and long-term empowerment for underprivileged women and children through scalable community-based educational systems and outreach programs.

Dhara Tattva

A sustainability and environmental systems initiative focused on organic waste management, ecological awareness, circular environmental practices, and scalable waste-processing solutions designed for real-world implementation.
