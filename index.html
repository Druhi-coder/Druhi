Research Snapshot  ·  CANary Pancreatic cancer detection  ·  CBC blood severity  ·  Biomarker analysis
Model Performance
AUC 0.98

GradientBoostingClassifier on Debernardi et al. 2020 dataset. AUC of 0.98 — near-clinical sensitivity at low false positive rates.

Biomarker Drivers
SHAP

CA19-9 and LYVE1 emerge as dominant predictive biomarkers. Interpretability is built in — not an afterthought.

CBC Feature Importance
Blood Severity

WBC count, platelet count, and hemoglobin dominate blood severity prediction — consistent with clinical literature. The model learns what hematologists already know.

Current limitation Dataset size constrains generalizability. Models trained on Debernardi et al. 2020 (590 samples). Expanding dataset scope is the active research problem.
