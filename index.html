ABOUT

I'm a student, researcher, builder and founder passionate about solving meaningful problems through technology. My work spans artificial intelligence, healthcare, education and sustainability, with projects ranging from AI-assisted cancer screening research and wearable computing to circular waste management and educational initiatives. Through CANary, AiR, DRiSe Foundation and Dhara Tattva, I enjoy building technologies that combine engineering with real-world impact while continuously exploring new ideas through research, writing and entrepreneurship.
