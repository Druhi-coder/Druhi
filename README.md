---

## Philosophy

Most of my work began without formal infrastructure, institutional access, or funding.

What started as curiosity gradually became a pattern:
- identifying difficult real-world problems
- teaching myself the tools required to approach them
- building systems around them anyway

A recurring theme across my work is reducing the distance between advanced knowledge and the people who normally cannot access it.
